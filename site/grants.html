How the numbers work

  • Civic Partners are organizations that run City-owned facilities or services at arm's length, such as the Library, the Zoo and Calgary Arts Development. The City lists four kinds of grant to them: operating, capital, community safety and innovation.
  • Calgary Arts Development passes at least 75% of its grant on to arts groups, so some of its money reaches other organizations.
  • FCSS is funded jointly by the Province of Alberta and the City and pays for preventive social programs run by local agencies. The 2027 and 2028 amounts cover only the programs funded for those years so far.
  • The City's pages only show the current funding years: for Civic Partners, the last year's actual amount and this year's budget. This site keeps every year it has collected, and when a budget year later gets an actual figure, the actual replaces it.
  • Recipient names are spelled differently from year to year on the City's pages. They're merged under the most recent spelling.
