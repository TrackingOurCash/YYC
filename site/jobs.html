How the numbers work

  • Daily tracking started –. The City's list shows no posting date, so a posting's date is the first morning it was seen.
  • Postings from before then were rebuilt from Internet Archive copies of the careers page, which caught it a few times a month. Their date is the first archived copy that lists them, and postings open only between copies are missed. These are marked "Archived".
  • Department, union, pay range, hours and position counts come from each posting's "Position and Pay Information" section. For archived postings that section usually wasn't saved, so department is "Not stated" and the position counts as one of unknown type.
  • Annual cost is the pay range midpoint × positions. When a posting's own pay isn't available, it uses the range for the same title on the City's compensation disclosure list, then the pay on another posting with the same title. Postings with no match count as $0, so totals are a floor. Hourly pay is annualised from weekly hours (35 if not stated), and temporary positions count at the yearly rate.
  • A posting is a job the City intends to fill, not a confirmed hire.
