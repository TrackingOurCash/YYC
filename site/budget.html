How the numbers work

  • Operating figures are expenditures net of recoveries, the City's own measure. Money one City unit charges another isn't counted twice.
  • Capital figures are the approved capital budget for each year. Projects approved now run as far ahead as 2032.
  • Actual spending is published only for the capital budget, for 2023 and 2024. The dataset has no approved capital figures for those two years.
  • "Change from previous year" compares the same department, figures type and budget.

Data: Calgary Open Budget on Open Calgary, refreshed daily. Code: github.com/TrackingOurCash/YYC.
