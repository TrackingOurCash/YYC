How the numbers work

  • Each ward office publishes a "Ward Budget & Expenditure Disclosure" report. Total spending includes staff salaries, which the City doesn't itemize to protect employees' privacy, so itemized expenses add up to much less than the total.
  • Reports cover the year to date. The latest one runs to –, so its totals are for part of the year; compare full years for a fair picture.
  • 2025 reports cover both the councillor who served until the October 2025 election and the one elected then.
  • "Community sponsorships" is the City's account for sponsoring individuals and organizations: event tickets, donations, and promotional items given out at events. Categories group the City's expense accounts; the account name is in each row.
  • Amounts are as reported, including refunds shown as negative amounts. The Mayor's office isn't included because no matching report was found.

Data: ward budget and expenditure reports on calgary.ca (Ward 1, 2026 for example) and Ward Boundaries on Open Calgary, refreshed daily. Code: github.com/TrackingOurCash/YYC.
