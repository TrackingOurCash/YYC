FCSS funding allocations

Family & Community Support Services (FCSS) partners with more than 100 social service program providers in Calgary. The services they offer address a wide range of issues, such as improving parenting skills, reinforcing positive child and youth development, increasing adult personal capital, strengthening positive social ties for vulnerable Calgarians and creating stronger neighbourhoods.
