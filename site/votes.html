How the numbers work

  • "Adds spending" marks motions that approve new or extra money: allocating, approving, drawing or investing a dollar amount, a budget increase, new one-time or ongoing funding, approving a budget or budget adjustments, or an amendment that raises an amount. Motions only asking for a report, referring money to a later budget, cutting or moving money already approved, borrowing, or putting money into a reserve aren't counted. The words that matched show when you hover the label. It's read from the motion's wording, so it misses some spending motions rather than count ones that aren't.
  • "Voted to add spending" counts a member's Yes votes on those motions, whether or not the motion carried.
  • "Mentioning money" means the motion's text includes a dollar figure or a word like budget, funding, grant, capital, levy, tax, contract or cost. It catches spending decisions but also some procedural motions; read the text to be sure.
  • The City's data has no motion number. Votes are grouped by meeting, date, wording and result, so two identically worded motions with the same result in one meeting may be counted as one.
  • Council terms start at the general elections of October 18, 2021 and October 20, 2025. Votes before October 7, 2020 aren't in the data; see the City's minutes for those.
