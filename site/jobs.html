How the numbers work

  • The City's list shows no posting date, so a posting's date is the first morning it appeared here. Tracking started –; earlier postings aren't counted.
  • Department, union, pay range, hours and position counts come from each posting's "Position and Pay Information" section.
  • Annual cost is the pay range midpoint × positions. Hourly pay is annualised from the posting's weekly hours (35 if not stated). Temporary positions count at the same yearly rate, so this is a yearly run rate, not the total paid over a term. Postings without pay count as $0.
  • A posting is a job the City intends to fill, not a confirmed hire.
