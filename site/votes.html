How the numbers work

  • "Mentioning money" means the motion's text includes a dollar figure or a word like budget, funding, grant, capital, levy, tax, contract or cost. It catches spending decisions but also some procedural motions; read the text to be sure.
  • The City's data has no motion number. Votes are grouped by meeting, date, wording and result, so two identically worded motions with the same result in one meeting may be counted as one.
  • Council terms start at the general elections of October 18, 2021 and October 20, 2025. Votes before October 7, 2020 aren't in the data; see the City's minutes for those.
